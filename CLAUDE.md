## Messages & notes for Johnny

When writing internal messages, notes, or handoffs for Johnny on the user's behalf:

- Write in the user's voice (first person, as if they wrote it).
- Concise and compact: lead with the point, no greetings or filler, no recap of what Johnny already knows.
- Low cognitive effort: short lines, bullets over paragraphs, one action per line, clear owner + deadline when relevant.
- Links and file paths inline, not explained.
- If it fits in 3 lines, use 3 lines.
