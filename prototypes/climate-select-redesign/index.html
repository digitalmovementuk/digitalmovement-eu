The problem with split systems

Traditional split systems don't suit every home

A standard split system needs an outdoor unit and a run of ducting or pipework across the outside wall. That can look untidy, add cost, and is often not allowed on properties with planning or leasehold restrictions.

Comparison of a traditional split system with a Climate Select self-contained system
Feature Traditional split system Climate Select self-contained system
Outdoor condenser unit Required Not needed
Ducting or pipework on the outside wall Yes None
Planning or leasehold restrictions Often not allowed Ideally suited
Appearance Visible run on the exterior Discreet, all components inside

Property-specific surveys

Every recommendation starts with a survey of your home

A system that works well in one room can underperform in another. Your surveyor looks at the factors that decide what will work for your property.

  • Glazing quality and window area
  • Solar gain and positioning
  • Insulation levels
  • Ambient noise
  • Room size and how it will be used
  • Outdoor access and installation logistics

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an outdoor unit?

No. Our self-contained systems house all components inside, so there is no outdoor condenser unit and no external refrigerant pipework.

Can the system heat as well as cool?

Yes. Our systems are reverse-cycle units that provide cooling in summer and heating in winter.

How much does it cost?

We give an indicative equipment cost before you instruct a survey. After the survey, and before you order, your surveyor confirms the project cost.

Is the home survey free?

We offer a free on-site survey, subject to conditions. We explain the conditions when you book.

Do I have to use your installation team?

No. Many customers use their own local tradespeople. Your surveyor confirms what your installer needs during the survey.

Which properties is it suited to?

Flats, garden rooms, offices, conservatories, granny annexes, listed buildings, and properties with planning or lease restrictions on external works. Each home is surveyed before we recommend a system.
