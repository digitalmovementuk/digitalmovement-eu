# Vertical compliance rules

Apply to all populated copy (Artifact B) and wireframe flags (Artifact C).

## Medical (HWG — Heilmittelwerbegesetz)

### Forbidden
- Curative promises: "garantiert", "100% Erfolg", "sicher heilbar"
- Outcome testimonials from patients ("Nach 3 Monaten war alles weg")
- Before/after images without explicit consent language
- Comparisons claiming superiority over other providers
- Fear-based language ("Ohne Behandlung drohen…")
- Misleading references to scientific studies

### Allowed
- Describing treatment methods factually
- Listing qualifications, certifications, equipment
- Process testimonials ("freundliche Beratung", "moderne Praxis")
- Educational content about conditions and modern approaches

### Safe phrasings
- "Wir unterstützen Sie bei…" (not "Wir heilen…")
- "Moderne Behandlungsansätze bei…"
- "Individuell abgestimmte Therapie"
- "Nach aktuellem medizinischem Standard"

### Focus areas (Privatleistungen)
Kassenzahnarzt work is low-margin. Page copy should lead with:
- Implantate
- Aligner / Kieferorthopädie
- Bleaching
- Ästhetische Zahnheilkunde
- Parodontologie

Separate landing pages per treatment, not one dental megapage.

## Legal (RVG — Rechtsanwaltsvergütungsgesetz)

### Forbidden
- "Günstige Anwaltskosten", "Rechtsberatung zum Tiefpreis"
- Fee undercutting framing
- Outcome guarantees ("Gewinnen Sie Ihren Fall")
- Success-rate statistics without strict context

### Allowed
- "Klare Kostenkommunikation"
- "RVG-konforme Abrechnung"
- "Kostenlose Ersteinschätzung" (only if genuinely offered)
- Describing specialisations and experience

### Channel-vertical fit
- Verkehrsrecht, Strafrecht — urgency-driven, Meta Ads convert fast
- Familienrecht — intent-driven, Google Search wins
- Skip corporate law landing pages — referral-driven market, not search-driven

## Consulting (Boutique-Beratungen)

### Voice rules
- Peer-to-peer, not vendor-to-client
- Founder credibility forward (BCG, McKinsey, Bain backgrounds)
- Methodology-specific, not generic "strategy"
- No "Marketing-Leitfaden" style

### Forbidden
- Generic consulting buzzwords: "Synergien heben", "Best-in-Class-Lösungen"
- Pure outcome claims without methodology backing
- Vendor-style CTAs ("Kostenlose Analyse!")

### Allowed
- Specific framework references (Porter 5 Forces, OKR, specific methodologies)
- Case structure references
- Founder bio + credentials block

### Target audience on page
- Managing Partners and Founders of 5-50 FTE boutiques
- Not marketing leads, not junior staff
- DACH-wide, geography less important

## NEO THE AGENCY (self)

### Services (fixed list)
- Paid Ads
- Website Development
- Search Engine Optimization (incl. AI Search)

### Voice
- "Wir" form (DE)
- Peer-to-peer (founder BCG background)
- Outcome framing
- No agency buzzwords ("volle Power", "kreative Explosion")

### Founder credibility block
Required in Block 4 (authority) on every master page:
- Founder name
- BCG background reference
- LinkedIn link
- Founder photo (asset placeholder)

### GDPR
All forms: explicit consent checkbox, privacy policy link.
Never auto-opt-in.

## General (all verticals)

- No "great", "amazing", "awesome" (EN) or "genial", "großartig" (DE)
- No exclamation marks in body copy
- No emojis anywhere in SEO copy
- Form fields: always explicit consent for GDPR
- Prices only if genuinely fixed, else "ab {{price}}" or skip
